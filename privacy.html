Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. About the Application

ISK PostgreSQL Backup is a private utility used to back up PostgreSQL databases and store encrypted backup files in Google Drive.

2. Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive authorization to create, upload, verify, download, and manage backup files created by the application.

Access is limited to the permissions granted by the Google account owner.

3. Data Security

Database backup files are encrypted locally before being uploaded to Google Drive. Encryption credentials are stored locally and are not published on this website.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal data or share Google account information with advertisers or third-party marketing services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until they are deleted by the owner or through an explicitly configured retention process. The owner can revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account settings.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application administrator using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
